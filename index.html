How to use Masroofy

1. First setup

  1. Create your account with your email and a password on the first screen.
  2. Open Account and set your currency (default EGP).
  3. Open Budget and type your salary for this month.

Other income (freelancing, bonuses)

  1. Open Budget and tap Add income under Other income.
  2. Enter the source (e.g. Freelancing), amount and date received, then tap Save income.

It adds to that month's income, your left to spend, your % budgets and your daily limit. Tap an entry to edit or delete it.

2. Cost centers (your budget partitions)

In Budget, each cost center has a name, a code or GL, a type and a color.

  • Fixed: the same amount every month, e.g. Fuel 2500.
  • % of income: a share of this month's salary plus other income, e.g. Savings 20%.
  • Split what's left into Savings puts any unassigned income into Savings.
  • Split income evenly gives every cost center an equal share.
  • Add cost center adds a new one. The × removes it.

3. Adding expenses

  1. Tap the green + button.
  2. Enter the amount, pick the cost center, and choose Cash or Card.
  3. Optional: change the date, add a GL/ref and a note.
  4. Tap Save expense.

Tap any expense in a list to edit or delete it.

4. Home screen

  • Left to spend: salary plus other income, minus everything spent this month.
  • Today: what you spent today against your daily limit.
  • Cash vs. card: how this month's spending splits between the two.
  • Cost centers: spent vs. budget for each one. Red means over budget.

5. Daily spending limit

Set it in Budget under Daily spending limit.

  • Auto: money left this month divided by the days left, recalculated every day.
  • Fixed: the same amount every day.
  • Off: hides the Today card.

If an expense takes you over today's limit, the app tells you by how much.

6. Debts

  1. Open Debts and tap Add debt (or the + button on that tab).
  2. Choose I lent or I borrowed, then enter the person, amount and an optional due date.
  3. When someone pays back part of it, open the debt, enter the amount under Record payment and tap Add payment.

A debt moves to Settled when it's fully paid. Overdue debts show in red. Debts don't count as spending.

7. Months and monthly close

  • Use ‹ and › at the top to move between months.
  • When a month is finished, open Budget and tap Close month. Its salary, budgets and expenses lock, like an accounting period close.
  • To change a closed month, tap Reopen month and enter your password.

8. Reports

  • Budget vs. actual: budget, actual, variance and % used for each cost center. Negative variance means overspent. Copy report as CSV lets you paste it into Excel.
  • Spending trends: a 12-month chart per category. Solid line is actual, dashed is budget. MoM compares with last month, YoY with the same month last year, and the trend tag shows if spending is rising.

9. Your data and login

  • Your data saves to your account online, about a second after each change.
  • The lock icon at the top logs you out.
  • Change your password in Account.

10. Your account

  • First time: enter your email and a password (6+ characters) and tap Create account.
  • After that, tap Sign in on any phone or laptop to see your data.
  • Each person has their own account. Nobody else can see your data.
